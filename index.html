For you, Amulu

Some people arrive in our lives like ordinary days. You arrived like a spell — suddenly the whole house had light in it, and I had a name I was proud of: Vadhina.

You were my first baby before I even met you. Before your voice, before your laugh, before the first time you held my finger — I already loved you, the way one loves a constellation before learning its name.

So today I don't wish you ordinary things. I wish you a life that feels like a castle with a thousand doors, and the courage to open every one of them. I wish you friends who stand beside you like a Patronus in the dark. I wish you a heart that stays soft even when the world is sharp, and a laugh that still sounds like the best kind of magic to everyone lucky enough to hear it.

Grow tall, grow brave, grow wildly. But no matter how far you fly — the door stays open, the lamp stays lit, and your Vadhina stays right here, cheering louder than anyone in the room.

With all my love, always
— Your Vadhina
